Overview

At Nestova Shop, we stand behind the quality of every product we sell. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return eligible items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

To start a return, please contact our support team within 7 days of receiving your order. Returns initiated after this window may not be accepted.

Return Eligibility

Items must meet the following conditions to be eligible for a return:

Returned within 7 days of delivery
Item is unused and undamaged
Original packaging and accessories included
Proof of purchase / order number provided
Item has been installed or used
Tampered seals or removed serial numbers
Missing original box or accessories
Return requested after 7 days

How to Initiate a Return

Follow these steps to return your item:

1
Contact Support

Email us at support@nestovaproperty.com or use the WhatsApp button on our site with your order number and reason for return.

2
Receive Return Authorisation

Our team will review your request within 24–48 hours and send you a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number and return address.

3
Pack & Ship the Item

Securely repack the item in its original packaging and ship it to the address provided. Write your RMA number on the outside of the package.

4
Refund Processed

Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within 3–5 business days to your original payment method.

Refund Timeline

After we receive and inspect your returned item, here's what to expect:

Day 1–2 — Inspection

We inspect the returned item for eligibility. You'll receive an email confirmation of receipt.

Day 2–3 — Approval

If the return is approved, we process the refund to your original payment method (bank transfer or card).

Day 3–5 — Refund Received

Your bank or card provider may take an additional 2–5 business days to reflect the credit in your account.

You will receive an email notification at every step of the refund process.

Defective or Wrong Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, we will cover the full cost of return shipping and send you a replacement or issue a full refund — no questions asked.

Free return shipping on all defective or incorrectly shipped items. Please report these issues within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

• Items that have been installed, used, or activated
• Products with removed or tampered security seals
• Items damaged by misuse, negligence, or improper installation
• Custom-ordered or special-order products
• Items returned without original packaging or accessories
• Returns initiated after the 7-day window

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any disputes arising from returns or refunds shall be resolved in accordance with Nigerian consumer protection regulations.

Need help with a return?

Our support team is available Monday – Saturday, 9am – 6pm WAT.
